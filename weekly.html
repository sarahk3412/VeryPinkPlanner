Weekly Planner

Keep it simple: 2–3 priorities per day. On mobile, these stack automatically.

Monday

  • Study block (60 min)
  • One important task
  • Self-care

Tuesday

  • Classes / notes
  • Assignment progress
  • Walk + water

Wednesday

  • Lab / practice
  • Clean inbox
  • Early night

Thursday

  • Review week tasks
  • Finish one deliverable
  • Stretch

Friday

  • Submit what’s due
  • Plan weekend
  • Reward yourself 💗

Saturday

  • Reset room
  • Meal / grocery plan
  • Fun hobby time

Sunday

  • Prep next week
  • Light review
  • Affirmations ✨
Tip: keep each day to 3 items max so you actually do it.
